PROJECT PRIMER (distilled from the archived audits; read this first)
================================================================================

WHAT THE SYSTEM IS.  A tabletop TAMP pipeline (PDDLStream + Fast Downward +
PyBullet, Franka Panda) for partially observable deterministic (POD) planning.
The contribution is the representation: an adaptive semantic partition of the
workspace into "Boxels" (object AABBs, occlusion-shadow volumes cast from a fixed
camera, octree-partitioned free space with face-merge), which makes occlusion
explicit symbolic state (Know-If fluents, blocks_view_at) so a classical planner
can search for hidden objects via optimistic determinization + replanning.
Baseline comparison: uniform voxel grid (~10x more cells/facts, worse success);
architectural comparison: TAMPURA (Curtis et al. 2024, arXiv:2403.10454).

KEY FILES (root):  boxel_env.py (env, scenes, camera, oracle, boxelization),
shadow_calculator.py, free_space.py, cell_merger.py, uniform_grid.py (baseline),
pddlstream_planner.py (+ pddl/), streams.py (grasp/kin/motion streams),
execution.py (pick/place/stack/sense execution), robot_utils.py, belief.py,
reboxelize.py, test_full_pipeline.py (main loop + eval verifiers), eval_runner.py,
eval_plotter.py, run_logger.py.  tampura_bridge/ runs OUR pipeline inside
TAMPURA's find_dice env (see #P4).  A src/ package refactor is planned but gated
(multi-commit branch, do only when the tree is otherwise quiet).

EVAL DATA.  Canonical headline dataset: eval_results/sweep_full_2026-05-28
(post CB#107 replan-cap removal; 810 cells = 3 goals x 3 variants x 30 seeds x 3
difficulty; 1800 s budget).  Thesis headline numbers (tab:headline): success
find-and-tray-stack 75.6/72.2/34.4 %, holding 65.6/66.7/47.8 %, stack
64.4/64.4/35.6 % (semantic / +mbs0.05 / uniform); stack-by-height semantic
100/80/13.3 % at h=2/3/4 (uniform 90/16.7/0).  NOTE: EVAL_SUMMARY.md documents
the OLDER sweep_anytime dataset (4500-row aggregated.csv, pre-cap-removal
numbers 42.3 % holding etc.) and is stale relative to the thesis; do not quote
it for the paper without saying which sweep it is.  A fresh full canonical sweep
(CB#113) is planned and should only run AFTER #P1-#P3 land, since those change
the system under test.

TAMPURA COMPARISON STATE.  TAMPURA source lives in WSL:
\\wsl.localhost\Ubuntu\root\tampura-work  (tampura/ core lib + tampura_environments/
incl. find_dice/ and panda_utils/; their .venv is python 3.11 / pybullet 3.2.7).
Local 20-seed TAMPURA find_dice re-run (runs/sweep_2026-05-26.json in WSL):
11/20 = 55 % success, 166 +/- 85 s per successful episode (published-implied
>= 63 %; published per-step 57 +/- 38 s, ours ~11 s/step).  The thesis frames this
comparison as INDIRECT and architectural (different task, codebase; local re-run
possibly disadvantages TAMPURA, TH#306).  Their hiding mechanic is CONTAINMENT
(die pose-aliased under a cup) vs our LATERAL shadows; their spawns are
hand-tuned reachable (x in [0.3,0.7], y in [-0.4,0.4], camera-visibility checked,
scene regenerated until the die is genuinely hidden); their controllers are
kinematic (motion via bundled BiRRT; grasp library: saved / antipodal / top).

================================================================================

(plan-execute-observe-replan over an optimistic determinization) with a genuine
POD planner: compile PDDLStream's sampled groundings into LW1 (Bonet & Geffner),
gaining branching contingent plans and deductive axioms.  Then a problem that
actually NEEDS it, e.g. a continuous/3D Wumpus-style domain.
WHY IT IS JUSTIFIED (the thesis's own disclosed gap, raised 3x by Till):
TH#238/#239/#248 (simplified POD, lacks deductive axioms, does not scale without
them); TH#307 (formal statement: we solve a single optimistic determinization
and never certify goal-belief containment b_G subset S_G as CLG/LW1 would);
TH#328 (pessimistic sensing would require exactly the LW1 axiom machinery we
lack; text reverted from thesis but the reasoning stands).  The unsound
3-strike bounded give-up (thesis §5 limitation) is the visible symptom.

geometry) is the natural first rung and doubles as an ablation of the semantic
partition.  Treat #P9 as post-deadline research unless #P1-#P4 finish early;
for the December paper it is future work, at most a positioning paragraph.
